3. What we do not do

  • We do not store IP addresses, only a country.
  • We do not locate you more precisely than a country.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • The listening history of an individual account is only opened by an authenticated administrator using two-factor authentication, and every opening is logged.
  • When an account is deleted, its plays are detached from it.

4. Cookies

This website sets no cookies. As with any website, our hosting providers see technical connection data such as your IP address and browser in their server logs. The home page may also ask our API for the latest tracks.

The listening app and the Creator Studio only use strictly necessary cookies: the sign-in session and, where a notice is shown, a technical cookie that remembers which version of our texts you accepted. There are no advertising cookies.

9. Advertising
