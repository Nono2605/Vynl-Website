How to release AI music in four steps.

  1. Create your artist page

    Pick a name, then add an avatar, a banner and a few words about you.

  2. Upload and declare

    Add your audio, group tracks into a release and declare how AI was used on each one.

  3. Send for review

    Tracks are reviewed before they go live in the catalog.

  4. Reach listeners

    Published tracks appear on Vynl, and your audience starts to build.

Before you publish AI music, a short questionnaire.

Trust runs both ways. Joining as a creator asks you for a few things up front.

  • Accept the Creator Terms. The rules of the road for publishing on Vynl.
  • Confirm you hold the rights to the content you will upload.
  • Tell us how you work with AI: fully generated, or mixed with your own craft.
