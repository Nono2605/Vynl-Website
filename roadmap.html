Where the beta stands, and what comes next.

Vynl is in beta. Follow what you can use today and what is still being built before it ends.

5 of 11steps are live

What you can use today.

Live in the beta, for listeners and for creators.

  • AI labels on every track

    Each track is labeled AI-assisted or AI-generated by its creator, and the label stays visible.

    Live

  • Listening app

    Listeners can play published AI music and follow the artists they like.

    Live

  • Uploads and releases

    Creators upload tracks, group them into releases and send them for review.

    Live

  • Artist pages and AI declaration

    An avatar, a banner and a bio for each artist, and a history of how AI was used on every track.

    Live

  • Catalog and audience analytics

    Creators see their catalog at a glance and how their followers grew.

    Live

Still to build before the beta ends.

The big pieces: how listeners pay, how artists get paid, and the infrastructure under it all.

  • Listening stats for creators

    Plays, unique listeners, top tracks and where the audience listens from.

    Coming

  • Subscriptions

    Listeners subscribe, and their subscription is what pays the artists they play.

    Coming

  • User-centric royalties

    Each subscription is shared by what that listener actually plays. The calculation and the reporting are still being built.

    Coming

  • Creator wallet and payouts

    Creators see what they earned and withdraw it.

    Coming

  • Database migration

    Moving the beta's data to production-grade infrastructure, for speed and reliability.

    Coming

  • End of the beta

    The beta closes once the steps above are done.

    Coming

No dates yet. The order can change as we learn from the beta. This page is updated as steps go live. For how royalties are meant to work, see Labels and royalties.
